## What was removed and why — read this before adding anything back

### Shutters: two entire ad groups, 15 keywords, deleted

The first draft had `Shutters - Gold Coast` and `Shutters - Brisbane` — the two most
expensive groups in the campaign at $6.22 and $6.49 average CPC, with terms like
*plantation shutters brisbane* ($6.96) and *shutters gold coast* ($8.10).

**Lusso does not sell plantation or timber shutters.** It sells **Thermalite** polymer
shutters. From `references/Business-facts.md`, confirmed by Jett on 12 Aug 2026, with an
explicit targeting ruling revised 1 Sep 2026:

> *Commercial / transactional — plantation shutters quote, plantation shutters near me,
> plantation shutters gold coast → **Still not targeted.** These people want a plantation
> quote specifically. Ranking for them buys enquiries that cannot be fulfilled and wastes
> fitting time.*
>
> *Never imply Lusso sells plantation shutters.*

That ruling was written about **organic** rankings. It applies far harder to paid, where
each of those unfulfillable enquiries costs $6–8 up front. The CRM corroborates it:
**zero** line items across 1,360 accepted-quote items in 24 months mention a shutter.

Shutter terms are now in the **negatives** list instead, which matters more than it looks:
without them, a phrase match on `blinds brisbane` will happily serve
*"plantation shutters blinds brisbane"*.

Also negative for the same reason: `ziptrak` (Lusso fits **ZipScreen**; Ziptrak is a
competitor brand it does not stock), `roller shutter`, `security shutter`, `louvre`.

### Motorised Blinds: held out, not deleted

10,480 volume at $2.44 — by far the cheapest clicks in the set, and the CRM shows
motorisation is genuinely core (remotes, Somfy/Acmeda hubs and motorised blind SKUs are
the most frequent line items in accepted quotes). But there is **no landing page for it**,
and the motorisation push was dropped on 2026-11-03. Pointing 10k of volume at a mismatched
page wastes the cheapest inventory available. Add the page, then add the group.

Volume-weighted CPC across this set is **$5.22**.

> the *whole* keyword table, which is dragged down by informational terms Lusso should not
> bid on. Real figure below.

| Landing page → enquiry | Enquiry → booked measure | Clicks/measure | **Cost per measure** |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2% (no price anchor) | 50% | 100 | **$522** |
| 3% (typical) | 50% | 67 | **$348** |
| 5% (good page) | 50% | 40 | **$209** |
| 8% (excellent) | 50% | 25 | **$131** |

Against **$1,474 gross profit** per appointment, every row is profitable. But top to bottom
is a factor of four, and the only lever you control is the landing page.

### What a budget buys, at the typical 3% row

| Monthly budget | Measures/month | Measures/week |
|---|---|---|
| $1,500 | 4.3 | 1.0 |
| $2,500 | 7.2 | 1.7 |
| $4,000 | 11.5 | 2.7 |

**The honest implication:** $1,500–2,500/month buys roughly **1–2 extra appointments a

— still profitable, but three times the number in the plan.

Start at **$2,000/month**, then scale on your own cost-per-measure instead of this table.

## Before you import — two things that are not optional

1. **Conversion tracking must exist first.** Without it Google optimises toward clicks,
   which is the one thing you are not buying. Create a conversion for the enquiry-form
   submission and import it into the Ads account.
2. **Lead source capture.** Already live in the CRM — the *How did they find us?* field on
   the customer record, plus automatic `gclid` detection on converted web leads. The habit
   is the remaining half: ask every caller. The **Ads** tab on the Growth Plan page shows
   coverage, and cost per measure is fiction until that number is high.

## Import steps

1. Create the account, set billing. *(Only you can do this — I can't enter payment details.)*
2. Install **Google Ads Editor**, sign in, download the account.
3. **Account → Import → From file**, in this order:
   - `keywords.csv`
   - `negative-keywords.csv`
   - `responsive-search-ads.csv`
4. Review the proposed changes, then **Post**.

### Settings Editor won't set — do these in the web UI

| Setting | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign type | **Search only** | Never let it opt into Display |
| Networks | Uncheck **Search Partners** and **Display** | Both dilute a local service campaign |
| Locations | Gold Coast + Brisbane, radius targeting | |
| Location option | **Presence**, not "presence or interest" | The default sends ads nationwide. The single most common way a local budget is burned. |
| Bid strategy | **Maximise clicks** + max CPC cap for 3–4 weeks, then **Maximise conversions** | Conversion bidding needs ~15–30 conversions of history |
| Budget | $65/day (≈$2,000/month) | |
| Ad schedule | Weekdays 7am–7pm, Sat 8am–4pm | An unanswered lead is a wasted click, and the business doesn't trade Saturdays |
| Location bid adjustment | **+20% on postcodes 4212 and 4223** | 4212 Hope Island averages $17,289 a job and 4223 Currumbin Valley $21,100, against $6,976 in 4217. From the Territory tab. |

## After two weeks

Ignore impressions, CTR and clicks. They are not the product.

1. **Cost per booked measure** — on the Growth Plan → Ads tab. Under $369 profitable,
   $369–884 tune it, over $884 the campaign is buying work you don't want.
2. **Search terms report** — the actual queries, not your keyword list. Every irrelevant one
   becomes a negative. Expect 20–40 more in month one; that is where the saving comes from.
3. **Which ad group produces measures**, not clicks.
4. **Lead source on the CRM record** vs what Google claims. Google over-attributes; the
   answer the customer gives on the phone is the truth.
